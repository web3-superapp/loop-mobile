# LOOP current product decisions

> Canonical current scope. Updated 2026-09-02.

This document applies to the Flutter source at the repository root. Material under `reference/legacy-prototype/` is frozen history and does not override these decisions.

## Product shape

- LOOP is a Flutter iOS and Android app. Web output is only a local UI-verification target.
- Decision 0110 (v3, 2026-10-08) replaces decision 0048's destinations with
  five primary destinations in this order: Chat, Square, Meme, Intel and
  Wallet (聊天 / 广场 / MEME / 情报 / 钱包). Meme is the post-login home (decision 0221; Chat was
  until then); 我
  (Profile) opens from the avatar on Chat rather than from the bottom
  navigation. The four v2 tabs `community`, `mining`, `launch` and `market`
  keep their routes and pages as ordinary child pages. Decision 0048's
  Community, Mining, Launch, Market and Wallet shell is history.
- `/home` and `/launchpad` are compatibility redirects to `/meme` (decision 0221) and
  `/launch`. Decision 0050 makes `docs/product/routes-manifest.json` (105
  routes since decision 0120, mirrored by
  `lib/core/navigation/route_manifest.dart`) the only route inventory; every
  unimplemented slug mounts a truthful pending surface and every retired
  location is logged and returned to Chat. The former
  103-surface catalog is read-only history, not a routing source or evidence
  that the 93 pages are complete.
- LOOP is spot-only. Perpetuals are disabled, have no primary-feature entry, and receive no further product work.
- Privy is the selected login, wallet and final authorization boundary.
- Reown AppKit 1.8.4 is accepted only as the connection and `personal_sign` transport for external EVM credentials under Privy. It is not a second identity system or a transaction wallet.
- The retained Hyperliquid Core perpetual implementation is disabled history and must not be relabelled as spot. Decision 0017 accepts a separate public Hyperliquid Testnet Spot market-discovery contract, and decision 0019 adds bounded public candles only after that discovery source admits the exact provider market. Spot execution remains unavailable and requires its own backend-mediated decision.

## Pay priority and delivery

Product priority and current delivery are separate:

- Product priority uses A, B and C. It is not a release phase or delivery promise.
- The retired catalog recorded 47 A-priority, 46 B-priority and 10 C-priority surfaces; the priorities below still describe Pay.
- B5 is A priority, B6 and B7 are B priority, and B8 is C priority.
- B5 through B8 are all deferred from the current release.
- Wallet keeps one Pay card labelled unavailable so the planned V2 product position remains visible after Home is retired.
- Every Pay route is informational only. It must not open a scanner, request camera access, collect an amount or recipient, select a payment provider, or submit a transaction.

## Launchpad delivery

- Launch remains the third first-class destination at `/launch`. All eleven
  Launch pages and all six Mining pages are mounted against the V2 launch,
  mining and referral modules (decision 0058); the non-actionable placeholder
  they replaced is retired with decision 0027.
- The mounted surface is an **off-chain catalogue and application flow**. The
  Launch contract baseline and the mining formula version are both undelivered,
  so every on-chain and formula-derived figure — the four state axes, price,
  caps, fees, supply, holders, graduation progress, power, output, rewards,
  rank and referral boost — renders the em dash with the server's own
  `reasonCode`, never `0` and never a fixture. Configuration slots without a
  confirmed version render "待确认（configVersion）".
- Catalogue segments come only from `scheduleStatus`, and `awaitingSchedule` is
  its own segment. "已毕业" is a liquidity-axis fact and stays unavailable; an
  ended schedule never implies graduation.
- `launch-apply` is a real owner-scoped resource: draft, compare-and-set edit,
  submit, and the server's six review states, with a returned application
  editable and re-submittable. Attachments and KYB have no provider and expose
  no upload control.
- Decision 0088 (S83c) reads loop-api decision 0076's unions: every on-chain
  Launch slot is either the byte-unchanged `unavailable` object or an
  `available` branch read from the contract at one block. The decoder picks the
  branch by the discriminator the old object already carried and decodes it
  strictly; the twelve S83a baseline responses decode exactly as before, and
  an `unavailable` `reasonCode` is any reason-code string. While no contract
  is configured every page renders as it did in step 7.
- On chain, `launch-detail` shows the four axes as four rows with the
  snapshot block and `stateTupleDigest`, and one combined sentence derived
  from them («销售成功，流动性准备中»; a pause is always stated on its own; no
  fifth state). `launch-rounds` shows each contract round's index, window,
  price, caps and raised amount plus the 13 `getSaleConfig` values;
  `launch-graduation` derives its four steps from `liquidityState` and
  `entitlementState` only, and says «已毕业» only at `LP_LOCKED`/`COMPLETED`;
  `launch-tier`, `launch-holders` and `launch-history` render their
  `available` branches, and only an indexed source can show an empty history
  as "no records". `loop-economy` has no `available` branch in the contract
  and is unchanged.
- `launch-trade` opens its main action only while the capability evidence is
  settled **and** the axes read `LIVE` + `ACTIVE`. It prepares one server
  intent (`POST /v2/launch/{id}/intents` → `201`), shows the intent's own
  review, and signs it through the one signing exit
  (`SigningIntent.backendCanonical`, `IntentKind.launchPurchase`, the server's
  transaction verbatim). A broadcast locks the attempt and is never reported
  as success; since decision 0089 its hash is reported to the server, whose
  `submitted` state is shown as is. Since decision 0090 the Privy signer admits
  the Launch slot for a Launch approval or purchase only, and only while
  `chain/status` publishes it. Refusals name the server's `reasonCode`, and
  an unexplained code is shown in a disclosure, never in the sentence. There
  is no sell side before graduation. `loop-stake` is non-executable as a
  whole page, and Launch eligibility does not depend on staking.
  `mining-rewards` disables its claim on the server's `claimExecutable:
  false`. No Launch surface other than `launch-trade` and no Mining path
  constructs a transaction or opens the signing sheet.
- Decision 0089 (S83c2) puts the USD1 approval in front of the purchase. The
  allowance is read only from `GET /v2/wallets/{id}/balances`
  (`launchChain.usd1`, strict); absent is 「授权状态未读取」 and closes buying.
  Short of the amount, the main action becomes 「先授权 USD1」: the existing
  wallet approval intent for exactly this amount (never unlimited — least
  exposure), USD1 on the Launch slot towards the Launch contract, through the
  one signing exit; the balances are then re-read until the allowance covers
  the amount. The three `409 INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE` refusals each have their own
  sentence and guidance, the broadcast hash goes to
  `…/intents/{id}/broadcast-report`, `launch-holders` counts 参与者 (distinct
  buyers), and the shared sign sheet's closing badge reads 「已广播」.
- Decision 0090 (S83e) lets the device sign on the Launch slot. privy_flutter
  0.10.1 has no chain-selection call and needs none: the native SDKs broadcast
  through the Privy wallet API on `eip155:<transaction.chainId>`, so the
  payload's own `chainId` (already required to equal the intent's) is the
  chain, and there is no wallet-level chain to switch back. The signer admits a
  non-primary chain only for `launchApproval` / `launchPurchase` and only when
  it equals `chain/status.launchChain.chainId`, read fresh for off-primary
  intents only (absent or unreadable admits the primary chain alone); send,
  approval, swap and perp stay `privy_chain_switch_unsupported` there. The
  transaction now reaches the SDK as the JSON string the native channels
  require, with `gas` spelled `gasLimit`, `type` as `2`/`0` and `null` keys
  omitted — no value changes, `privy_payload_unencodable` before the wallet
  otherwise. Device evidence is still pending.
- `referral` reads `GET /v2/referral`: one invite code per account, per-level
  counts grouped by the server's `validationStatus`, and an unavailable boost.
  Only `valid` edges count as effective relationships. Every figure is a Mining
  Power input — never a commission, a payout or a downline income.
- A community wears one face everywhere it is named. Decision 2026-09-21
  answers a real-device report that no community icon was visible: the server
  publishes twelve presets `avatar:preset/community-01..12`, the frozen
  prototype drew four of them, and the eight without an image were rendered as
  identical grey initials — while every page except the Community home drew
  initials even for the four that did have one. The community atlas
  `assets/communities/loop-community-atlas.webp` is now 4x3: the four
  prototype illustrations unchanged in row 0 (LOOP, PEPE, BONK, MCAT) and
  eight geometric Lime badges for 05..12, whose SVG sources are kept in
  `assets/communities/src/` and are not bundled. One widget,
  `CommunityLogo`, draws every community identity in the app — home list,
  discovery, the record folio and its identity row, the member directory,
  search results, the message centre, forward targets, the mining community
  board and one community's mining panel — so a community cannot show one face
  on one page and another face on the next. A community whose `logoRef` is
  absent or unresolvable still gets an identity rather than a placeholder: its
  own initials on one of six grounds mixed from Lime, Chalk, Muted and Ink,
  chosen by a stable hash of the community id, never of its name. No fill in
  that table may match a surface the tile lands on, which the ground probe and
  `community_logo_test.dart` both hold it to.

## Communication

- Stream Chat + Stream Video/Audio Rooms is the selected communication integration.
- The production Chat inbox uses Stream's official client, persistence, token provider, channel-list controller/view, channel scope and message page. Stream owns messages, pagination, ACK/read state, presence, typing and offline synchronization.
- `communicationGatewayProvider` remains the fail-closed seam for preview-only secondary communication surfaces that have not yet moved to official SDK UI. Only `lib/main_preview.dart` injects memory data; `lib/main.dart` never injects it.
- Communication mode is explicit: memory data is `preview`; the Stream seam is `production`. Preview UI continuously identifies itself as offline, simulated and not connected.
- The official client may be constructed from the public API key without connecting. A production session source must obtain a backend-derived Stream user ID and short-lived server-issued token; missing or failed authorization prevents controller/message UI from mounting.
- Privy user ID is only an opaque logout/account-switch key in Flutter. It is never converted into the Stream user ID. Each principal gets an isolated Stream client/persistence pair; logout and account switches retire the old pair, invalidate in-flight authorization, and disconnect without deleting per-user offline history.
- Text chat composition is wired through official UI. Attachments and voice recording remain disabled until platform permissions, upload policy and provider/device verification are complete.
- Incoming Stream `token_card.v1` attachments are the narrow exception to disabled attachment composition: official Stream message rendering may display their strict identifier-only reference. A compliant producer/server contract forbids persisting price, liquidity, risk, ownership, provider links, watch state, or trading actions; the receiver hides violations without claiming they were never stored.
- The Token Card renderer is synchronous and fail-closed. Missing, extra, malformed, repeated, mixed, or unknown-version payloads show no supplied facts. Valid cards remain `Current facts unavailable` until a separately freshness-bounded backend facts projection exists; preview facts stay visibly marked `开发预览` and cannot mutate a watchlist or trade.
- Raw Token Cards are intercepted before Stream's computed link/media type can select a default renderer, and standard top-level URL/action/display fields are rejected. Compact message and draft previews replace every raw Token Card with a fixed safe label so captions, titles, URLs, and mutable facts cannot escape through channel, reply, thread, or search previews. Outgoing Token Card composition remains disabled until a backend or Stream before-send policy enforces the same complete wire shape; Flutter receive validation alone cannot guarantee stored data.
- The legacy group, direct-message, group-information, request, search, and token/facts preview surfaces remain Preview-only. Step 4 gave the four conversation slugs their V2 production pages, so the route picks the labelled fixture page only in Preview mode; production navigation can never mount a fixture conversation.
- The legacy E9 asset-message route remains Preview-only but is now Spot-only: every fixture fact is visibly labelled, no position/trade/copy/save/alert capability is implied, its sole enabled action opens `/market`, and Watch stays disabled. Generic internal names such as `assetSnapshot` remain implementation labels and do not authorize Perp semantics.
- Legacy Message Requests remain process-local Development Preview state. Accept removes one pending fixture without creating or opening a Stream conversation, Ignore causes no sender interaction, and Report removes the fixture without submitting moderation. Unknown or already resolved IDs fail, pending actions are single-flight per card, and the Inbox count derives from the current Preview projection rather than a fixed badge.
- Legacy Preview message conversations now use one exact registered `conversationId` across Inbox navigation, group/direct pages, group information, and scoped search. Missing, repeated, unknown, or ID/kind-mismatched targets show an unavailable surface and the memory gateway never falls back to another fixture. Production remains on Stream's official full-CID route; Audio Room target selection remains backend-owned.
- Legacy Preview group-information switches change only labelled process-local layout state and never claim Stream notification persistence. Turning off the parent example clears its mentions-only dependent state. Member operations and Leave remain disabled until an official Stream mutation is connected; closing a dialog is not a group-membership change.
- Chat exposes one fixed create menu and Profile exposes `我的好友`; production now uses the reviewed principal-bound LOOP social adapter for relationship-aware Alias-prefix search, accepted friends, incoming/outgoing requests, request decisions and backend-created group/direct channels. `public_profile_id` is the only command target, `profile_code` is the immutable display discriminator, and mutable/non-unique account Alias rows remain valid. Wallet addresses, Privy/LOOP IDs, Stream user IDs and group aliases never become public-search or client-selected membership identifiers.
- Friend/request decisions remain server facts. Each mutation uses a new UUIDv4 as both `Idempotency-Key` and `operation_id`; an ambiguous or malformed-success POST is queried before the exact request can be replayed, while anything except the exact route-specific operation-not-found response never authorizes a duplicate. Group/direct `202` operations poll at the greater advertised delay under monotonic time and attempt ceilings, and `operator_required` stops rather than creating a second channel. UUID/body retention is currently process-local; process-death recovery needs a separately reviewed encrypted journal or backend owner-scoped discovery contract.
- Groups submit 2–29 accepted friends' `public_profile_id` values and accept only a matching `group_id`, unordered friend set and canonical `messaging:loop_group_*` CID; direct creation converges on the backend's fixed `messaging:loop_direct_*` CID. The returned CID still passes the exact online Stream CID + current-member gate before official channel UI mounts. Stream remains the truth source for messages and realtime channel state; Flutter never creates channels, chooses Stream identities or mutates membership directly. The labelled memory Preview still returns no Stream CID.
- Social Privacy is separate from public Profile Privacy and controls only new friend requests, group invites and direct messages with fail-closed defaults. A group Alias is an immutable `group_id`-scoped pseudonym; direct channels have no such namespace, peer search returns only `group_alias_id` plus Alias, and LOOP makes no strong-anonymity claim. Group Stream presentation uses only the exact current-member v1 projection and otherwise the neutral `成员` label across messages, references, mentions, reactions, threads, typing, list previews and avatars; it never falls back to an account-level name or stable Stream ID. Amended 2026-09-20 (decision 0055): a direct channel does not fall back to one either — Stream holds no name for a LOOP account, so `User.name` reads back as the internal `User.id`; a direct bubble carries no sender name and a direct mention candidate carries the peer's public `alias ?? loopId`, the same source the DM header already uses. Amended 2026-09-20 (R13-5): a group or community composer no longer hides every `@` candidate. It replaces Stream's mention overlay — which searches and types the account identity — with LOOP's own, where a candidate is resolved, prefix-filtered and typed as the channel Alias, a member without a projection and the member themselves are absent, and the Stream mention link still leaves with the message as an id list. Amended 2026-09-29 (decision 0107, S102): "current member" means the channel's current Stream member row, not only the bounded slice Stream loads with the channel — a room is opened with Stream's 100-member ceiling and a sender outside it is looked up by Stream user id in deduplicated, debounced batches (never by a typed Alias), through the same fail-closed projection check; a failed lookup is retried twice and then left at 「成员」, and a departed member returns to 「成员」 at once. New groups use the receipt's `group_id`; after restart an already membership-gated Stream group may resolve its validated channel ID through the backend without an idempotency key, while known direct IDs fail locally. Friend deletion, block/unblock, QR discovery, wallet-address search and group-member administration remain out of this slice.
- Stream Video now has a foreground-only, principal-bound SDK lifecycle and a narrow backend identity/token seam. The SDK is constructed only after an initial backend token is available, connects with push registration disabled, and is retired on route disposal, logout, or account change.
- Production voice surfaces never render preview room/member data. Audio Room now has a foreground lobby, backend-authorized target seam, muted single-flight join, official `CallState` participant/capability/media UI, explicit Speak action and principal/target/client cleanup. Missing Video tokens or room locator keep the join action disabled.
- Decision 0055 makes every Audio Room a community resource: the lobby is opened from a community record, not from the generic Chat inbox, because a room without a community has no locator, and the authorized room is handed to the reviewed lobby as a constructor argument rather than through a scoped provider. While the `voiceRooms` capability's provider-evidence flag is pending, `voiceroom` and `voiceroom-full` render an explanation and issue no request at all. The room resource carries no speaker directory, so removing a speaker is unavailable; a hand raise is a request to speak and never a removal target.
- A chat notification that names a channel LOOP cannot map produces no navigation intent at all, and the `/chat/channel/:cid` deep link records the unmatched location before returning to Community. A terminal `operatorRequired` outcome is shown as an unresolved result with no retry: retrying would open a second operation.
- Decision 0055 connects the V2 `communication` module. A conversation opens only through a server-issued CID: `chat.channelCid` for a community, the `directGetOrCreate` operation result for a DM, the channel list or a notification for a group. `chat.status` has three values, and `syncing` renders "聊天权限同步中" rather than unavailable. A channel's LOOP-assigned prefix is the only thing that picks a surface, shared by notifications, chat search and the retained `/chat/channel/:cid` redirect.
- Chat search, forwarding and merging are Stream SDK operations with no LOOP backend. Search stays inside the channels the account can read and never enters `/v2/search`; a group or community result carries the neutral member label rather than an account-level Stream name. Forwarding caps at 20 messages, skips and counts a deleted or empty one, queries its source conversation by exact CID, and targets only one bounded page of already-joined channels, which the page states. Decision 0056 exports the merged transcript by capturing the rendered anonymous card, encoding it to PNG on device and handing it to the system share sheet through `share_plus` 12.0.2; nothing is uploaded and nothing is kept.
- Stream user tokens are minted through `POST /v2/chat/token` and `POST /v2/video/token` with the contract headers and a per-attempt idempotency key. Decision 0045's budget is unchanged: one 401 refresh and one bootstrap recovery, no caching and no persistence.
- `group-info` shows member management, the group profile and notification preferences as unavailable, and leaves a group only through the LOOP backend, never Stream's own `leave`. `community-ai` restores its layout with every functional area closed by the server's `COMMUNITY_AI_RUNTIME_DEFERRED`.
- `dm` opens only after a friendship exists. Without one it offers exactly one next step, `POST /v2/message-requests`, and states that an unreachable target answers the same way as a non-existent one, so nothing about the other account is inferred. No surface claims end-to-end encryption.
- Audio Room uses only Stream's `audio_room` type. The backend must pre-create the room, add the server-derived Stream user and assign a role without `create-call`; the app never calls `getOrCreate`. This server permission is mandatory because Stream Video 1.4.3 internally sends `create: true` during `join()`.
- A first join disables camera, microphone and screen share, while playback uses the reader's output choice (speaker by default, decision 0106). `Joined` is presented as media connecting; only official `Connected` state is Live and eligible for Speak.
- Decision 0106 (S101) supersedes "foreground-only": a voice room keeps playing and a speaker keeps being heard while LOOP is in the background (iOS `audio` background mode; Android Stream call service with an ongoing 「正在语音房 · 社区名」 notification whose action is 离开). A call the provider gave up on is joined again automatically with back-off until 离开, the room ending or the membership going; the reader picks 扬声器 / 听筒 in the room's control bar; and `voiceroom-full`'s header entry is offered only to a host with host controls (deep links still open it).
- Audio Room owns microphone-only native configuration plus the background-audio pieces decision 0106 names. Backgrounding (`paused`/`hidden`/`inactive`) is not a cleanup. Every terminal cleanup — 离开, the room ending, an account change, `detached` — immediately starts native suspension, terminal mute, and one single-flight leave without letting a possibly stuck Speak/Mute/native future delay leave. Cleanup succeeds only after the retired object disappears from Stream's `activeCalls`, earlier microphone work settles, and a second post-command mute runs; fast resume and rejoin remain blocked until then, and a completed failure can only be retried. Version 1.4.3 permits one Speak start per Call, so Mute requires leave/rejoin before another Speak and the unsafe stopped-track recreation path is never entered. The flow never uses Stream's track auto-restore path. The auto-registering Stream Video Push plugin is not linked; optional Android incoming-call/Telecom/screen-share components and the phone-call/camera/projection service types are removed from the merged app manifest (the call service itself stays, decision 0106), and iOS has no PushKit, CallKit or VoIP entitlement configuration — its only background modes are `audio` and `remote-notification`. Audio Room still requests no camera: the app's single camera declaration (`android.permission.CAMERA`, `NSCameraUsageDescription`) belongs to the chat composer's 拍照 row (decision 0074), and no camera hardware feature is declared.
- The current Flutter code contains a shared LOOP identity Bootstrap source, strict Chat/Video user-token loaders, and a production Audio Room join path, but no production room locator. The token loader caches no Stream token, allows at most one 401 refresh and one bounded bootstrap recovery, and never makes provider connectivity a local fact. It does not prove that chat writes, presence, push, ringing, media, or voice rooms are live.
- A provider-neutral `notification.v1` classifier and root coordinator now fix the future navigation contract without enabling Firebase. Production still uses a disabled EventSource. The coordinator derives identity only from the real session plus verified bootstrap Stream user, retains at most the latest interaction for a bounded restore window, and revalidates before resolving an official Chat CID, the Audio Room lobby, or the truthful notification center. Foreground/background delivery never navigates. This does not parse raw Stream/Firebase payloads, register devices, initialize providers, ring, or claim delivery.
- Notification interaction deduplication is bounded and process-local. Cross-isolate/restart delivery and ordinary-push/VoIP ringing remain disabled until a persistent atomic receipt design, exact provider names, real payload fixtures, and native routing policy are approved.
- A persistent group with 200,000 members is not a verified Stream capability. LOOP must obtain written provider confirmation covering membership, message semantics, reactions, mentions, history, search and moderation before promising it.
- Without that written confirmation, the product must adopt a sharded-group or channel model and update the information architecture before implementation.

## Identity and safety language

- An internal immutable `user id` is the primary identity for accounts, social relationships and communication-provider mapping.
- Privy remains the only identity source. Email OTP, Google OAuth and iOS-only Apple OAuth are login methods; an external EVM wallet may use Privy SIWE to log in while signed out or link to the exact current fully verified principal. OAuth linking is not part of this slice.
- Client-visible provider configuration is supplied only through the centralized `AppConfig` plus build-profile JSON. Debug/Profile and Release declarations must match their Flutter mode; a mismatch gates every provider-backed capability. This profile is not a Production/Mainnet switch. Privy/Stream/OAuth secrets, Apple/APNs `.p8`, Sentry auth tokens, AppsFlyer server secrets, Firebase service accounts, wallet private keys, provider tokens and refresh tokens remain server/provider-owned and absent from Flutter.
- Reown is constrained to canonical `eip155` accounts and the exact Privy-generated message sent through `personal_sign`. AppKit auth, Email, Social, Embedded Wallet, SIWE, analytics, Link Mode, Solana and transaction authority are disabled, and the connection is disposed after each attempt.
- External EVM addresses are listed only as linked Privy sign-in credentials. They do not enter `PrivyWalletSummary`, `WalletSigningGateway`, balances, assets, transaction review or spot execution; the current Privy Embedded Ethereum wallet remains the sole mobile wallet-identity candidate for future backend-authorized trading.
- Signed-out SIWE selects login; an already authenticated principal selects link and may not fall back to login. Link completion is principal-bound, and LOOP does not automatically transfer, unlink, delete or merge another user's credential.
- Privy OAuth callbacks use `com.cywd.loop.privy`; Reown wallet returns use `com.cywd.loop.wallet`. Android routes them to separate activity owners, and Apple OAuth remains iOS-only behind the Sign in with Apple entitlement.
- The native client now has a principal-bound adapter for the implemented `POST /v1/bootstrap` boundary. It sends one current Privy Bearer access token, accepts only the backend-derived LOOP and Stream identities, retries one HTTP 401 at most once with a newly requested token, and keeps the validated identity in memory only.
- Each newly accepted fully verified Privy login may eagerly start that bootstrap once for its principal when a safe backend origin is configured. The request is non-blocking and its absence or failure does not reject authentication; a same-principal credential link is not another login. This is the narrow trigger refinement accepted by decision 0043.
- Production Dio construction uses two exact-origin profiles rather than one universal client. Public Hyperliquid Testnet reads use one credential-free HTTPS client that rejects Authorization and redirects before dispatch. The LOOP backend uses its own HTTPS-or-loopback-Development client; its Privy Bearer remains request-local. Both profiles reject Cookie, Proxy-Authorization, and `X-Api-Key` request headers, and neither logs payloads or credentials, persists cookies, or automatically retries requests.
- The credential-free public profile is not a provider permission surface. Current Hyperliquid mobile adapters remain Testnet `/info`-only and never call `/exchange`; the backend profile also rejects Authorization stored in Dio defaults so a Privy Bearer cannot persist beyond the owning request.
- Chat and Video share that trusted `stream_user_id` projection and now load their distinct short-lived user tokens from the LOOP backend through one principal-bound, non-persisting loader. One 401 refresh and one bootstrap recovery are independently bounded; key/user/expiry/no-store drift fails closed. Actual provider connection, refresh, channels, messages and Video behavior remain device-unverified, while Audio Room still lacks a production room locator.
- Wallet addresses are bindable and replaceable credentials. They are not database primary keys, public chat identities or communication user IDs.
- Wallet readiness is mounted directly from the current verified Privy session. The app may create the first Embedded Ethereum wallet and display/copy only a complete address, while malformed, Preview, or unverified values fail closed. Address presence never enables balances, deposits, QR funding instructions, Send, Swap, signing, or transaction claims; Manage wallets contains no fixture identities.
- Wallet prototype routes never fill missing context with ETH, a fixture wallet, or a generated signing intent. Asset details consume one typed labelled fixture, Signing Review requires an originating intent object, and the disabled DApp layout can project only the current verified Privy wallet identity. Catalog descriptions report Preview and unavailable capabilities rather than product priority as delivery evidence.
- Providerless Wallet drafts use exact typed state. Transfer amount syntax mirrors only the published positive-decimal String envelope and preserves the accepted source unchanged; it is not balance, precision, recipient, fee, or canonical-intent evidence. Every Swap projection consumes one immutable labelled snapshot, which any edit invalidates until an explicit atomic restore. Local revisions, quote layouts, and reviews never enable HTTP, idempotent transfer submission, wallet handoff, or success claims.
- Providerless Wallet controls now have observable local semantics: History and testnet filters change only labelled Preview rows, revocation is disabled, and Bridge progress consumes a typed immutable Preview snapshot. Naked Bridge progress routes fail closed, while result-state examples continue to say that no transaction was submitted. None of these controls proves wallet network support or provider activity.
- LOOP does not present an AI Guard brand or a proprietary risk score.
- User-visible copy is written for the person using the app, never for the backlog (decision 0070). A screen never renders a delivery step id (`D19`), a rule id (`rule:verified-members-v1`), a `reasonCode`, a capability id or an environment-variable name, and never uses the internal words 口径 / 观测 / 投影 / 聚合. A closed surface states what cannot be done now in one sentence, with at most one more for when or how it will work, and it promises nothing; the implementation reason moves into the details block or is dropped. Attribution is unchanged: `来源 …`, `观察于 …`, `区块高度 …` and `规则版本 …` remain as fine print beside the figure they qualify, which is where technical detail is allowed to live. Truthfulness is unchanged too — unreadable never becomes zero, safe, or "no records". `docs/copy-glossary.md` holds the banned list, the replacement table and the sentence patterns, and `check_user_visible_copy` in `scripts/check_harness.py` fails the build on the machine-checkable half, allowlisting wire constants, `reasonCode` keys, comments and diagnostics.
